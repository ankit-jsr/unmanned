**Reviewer 1**

**Comment R1.1:** "Please report how many participants were excluded for insufficient actigraphy data."

**Classification:** Accept

**Response:** We agree that the number of participants excluded for insufficient actigraphy data should be reported. We have added this information to Section 2.2: 27 participants were excluded because they had fewer than 5 valid nights of actigraphy data.

**Comment R1.2:** "The adjustment set seems thin. Did you consider caffeine intake?"

**Classification:** Partial

**Response:** We thank the reviewer for raising caffeine intake as a possible confounder of the association between sleep duration and exam performance. We did not collect caffeine intake data in this study, so we cannot add it to the adjustment set. However, we agree that this is a relevant gap, and we now acknowledge the absence of caffeine data as a limitation of the study in Section 4.3, paragraph 2.

- Section 2.2: added the number of participants excluded for insufficient actigraphy data (27 participants with fewer than 5 valid nights).
