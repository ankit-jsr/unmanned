The blank doc problem

You know you need to write an issue this week. You have a topic — something vague like "AI in the workplace" or "why I changed my mind about X." You open a blank doc. An hour passes.

The writing isn't the hard part. The plan is the hard part: exactly what to say, the specific insight that makes this issue worth reading, the structure that gets from hook to CTA without losing people.

That 20-minute planning step is what separates a focused, shareable issue from something that meanders. This skill does that step for you.

What the skill does

  1. Paste your topic or idea Can be rough. "I want to write about how most creators undercharge for sponsorships" is enough to start.
  2. Get a sharpened, arguable claim Not a theme — a specific point someone could agree or disagree with. The skill shows you where it started and what it arrived at.
  3. Get the full plan in one output Three hook/headline options (direct, curiosity, contrarian), a 3-section structure with word counts, one CTA, and three subject line variants.
Example — The Operator newsletter, sponsorship pricing
Sharpened topic

Newsletter operators with under 5,000 subscribers systematically undercharge sponsors because they price on raw CPM rather than on the demonstrably higher conversion rates small, trusted lists deliver.
