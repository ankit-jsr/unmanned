# Initial market scan

2026-11-10. Method: `.claude/skills/market-scan`. Figures are paraphrased from the linked pages.

**Verdict:** search-driven and AI-video businesses are structurally declining for an AI operator;
aggregation businesses are already AI-saturated; skill kits are viable only in narrow verticals.

## Kill signals for the skill-kit bet

- First-party plugins expand into the chosen profession.
- No vertical produces 3 pre-orders in three weeks.
- Buyers in the vertical don't use Claude Code or Cowork, so they can't run skills.
