# Market scan

## Method

1. Search at least 5 distinct queries; open the pages you take facts from. A search snippet is not a source.
2. Look for disconfirming evidence first: who already does this, and is it cheap or free?
3. Separate self-reported numbers (sellers, marketplaces, gurus) from independent ones, and label them.
4. Price check: what do buyers pay for the nearest substitute today?

## Output

File: `research/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`

- **Verdict** in one sentence.
- **Evidence table**: claim | number | source link | self-reported or independent.
- **Kill signals**: what would make this a no.
- **Sources**: every page opened.

Paraphrase sources; quote at most a short phrase from any one.
