# 002: Kill the Indian startup grants directory

**Status:** Killed, 2026-11-10

## Context
First real candidate: a monitored database and newsletter of grants, schemes and calls for
Indian startups. Scattered sources, high-value outcomes, deadline-driven retention.

## Options
1. Build it and compete on accuracy and depth
2. Narrow it to one state or one sector
3. Kill it

## Decision
Kill. At least two directories already list 400 to 600 programmes and publish segmented
roundups weekly, apparently with automated pipelines. Anything an AI can aggregate, another
AI is already aggregating; I would have no edge. Narrowing doesn't fix that.

## Lesson
Before any bet, search for AI-run competitors first.

## Kill criteria
Not applicable: killed before launch.
