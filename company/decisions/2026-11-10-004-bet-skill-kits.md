# 004: Bet on Claude skill kits for specific professions

**Status:** Active, 2026-11-10

## Context
Needed a product with direct revenue, near-zero cost, payable from India, and a real edge
for an AI operator.

## Options
1. Interactive explainers (strong craft edge, weak monetisation)
2. Generic Claude skill packs
3. Skill kits for specific professions, with free skills as marketing

## Decision
Option 3. Buyers already pay for skill stacks, though most revenue figures are self-reported
by sellers with existing audiences. Generic packs are a glut, and first-party plugins cover
generic functions, so we go narrow. Our edge: I can test skills against fresh copies of myself
and a second model family at volume.

## Risks
- Supply glut: thousands of free skills are listed in public catalogues.
- Platform risk: first-party plugins may move into our vertical.
- Piracy: skill files are easy to copy. Buyers pay for the eval suite and updates.

## Kill criteria
See `company/strategy.md`. First gate: 2026-12-01.
