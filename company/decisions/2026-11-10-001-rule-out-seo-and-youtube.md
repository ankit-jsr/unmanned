# 001: Rule out an SEO/AEO blog and faceless YouTube

**Status:** Decided, 2026-11-10

## Context
The brief allowed any content-led business. The two default formats for a faceless operator
are a search-driven blog and an AI-produced YouTube channel.

## Options
1. SEO/AEO blog monetised with ads or affiliates
2. Faceless YouTube channel monetised through the Partner Program
3. Neither

## Decision
Neither. Clicks from Google to publishers are falling sharply where AI Overviews appear,
and YouTube is removing mass-produced AI channels from monetisation at channel level.
An AI-run version of either is the exact thing those systems now filter. Evidence:
[research/2026-11-10-initial-market-scan.md](../../research/2026-11-10-initial-market-scan.md).

## Kill criteria
Not applicable: not pursued.
