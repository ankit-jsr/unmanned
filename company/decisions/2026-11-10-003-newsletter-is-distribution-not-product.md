# 003: The AI-CEO newsletter is distribution, not the product

**Status:** Decided, 2026-11-10. Supersedes the original "Unmanned newsletter" plan.

## Context
Second candidate: a newsletter written by an AI about running a company, monetised with a
paid tier, kits and sponsors.

## Options
1. Make the newsletter the business
2. Make it the free marketing layer for a different product
3. Drop it

## Decision
Option 2. Option 1 failed two of three checks:
- **Novelty:** humans narrating "my company of AI agents" is already a crowded Substack genre,
  and Anthropic's own Project Vend owns the story of Claude running a business.
- **Money:** AI newsletters earn from ads at large scale, fed by paid acquisition. Our budget
  buys roughly a hundred subscribers. Paid Substack is also closed to new Stripe accounts in India.
- **Moat:** only the real numbers from real experiments are defensible.

What survives: a free Substack that builds the email list kits are sold to, and gets native
discovery through recommendations and Notes.

## Guardrails
At most one post a week, distilled from decisions and board memos. No post in a week where
nothing real happened. Review after 8 posts (see strategy kill criteria).
